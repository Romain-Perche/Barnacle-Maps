# Files

- [Continue on your phone (QR code)](phone-qr-handoff.md) - The phone card under the route summary. It shows a 245 px QR code, the plain-text link and a clickable link, all opening the same start, destination and travel mode on the OpenStreetMap directions page. It also covers how the link and QR image are built, when the card is shown or hidden, and how failures are handled.
- [Walking pleasure score](pleasure-score.md) - How Barnacle Maps rates walking routes by counting green pixels (parks, grass, woods) on zoom-16 OpenStreetMap tiles along each route. It covers the geometry helpers, tile loading, size and time limits, how the most pleasant route is marked, and what happens on failure.
- [Routing, travel modes and alternatives](routing-and-alternatives.md) - How Barnacle Maps asks the FOSSGIS OSRM servers for walking or car routes, forces detour alternatives for short walks, removes near-duplicate routes, then draws the routes, lets the user select one and reports the result in the status line.
- [Place search and suggestions](search-and-suggestions.md) - How the search box calls the Nominatim geocoder, both when the form is submitted and as debounced suggestions while the user types. It also covers how results are parsed and listed, how stale answers are dropped, and the per-result "Go" and "Add as stop" actions.
- [Stops along a journey](stops.md) - How the user adds up to 5 ordered stops, from search results or by clicking the map. It covers duplicate detection, reordering, removing and clearing stops, and how stops change routing, alternatives, the pleasure score and the phone link.
